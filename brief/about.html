About

Marketing news that matters.

Nova Brief is a marketing publication from Nova Social. It covers the campaigns, stories, stats and ideas worth knowing about — without the waffle.

There is no shortage of marketing news. There is a shortage of marketing news you can read in five minutes and come away actually knowing something.

That is the job Nova Brief is trying to do. Every week we pull together the campaigns that are worth studying, the industry moves that change how people work, the numbers worth quoting, and a short, honest view on what it all means.

What to expect

A fast Daily Brief of the stories that moved something. A Campaign of the Week broken down properly — what happened, why it matters, our take. Killer Stats worth remembering. And The Nova Take, where we say what we think.

Who makes it

Nova Brief is written and edited by the team at Nova Social, a marketing agency that helps businesses build smarter, simpler marketing. The publication is editorially independent of the agency's client work — when a story touches a Nova Social client or partner, we say so.

A note on how it's made

We use tooling — including AI — to help monitor sources and prepare research and first drafts. Nothing publishes automatically. Every story is reviewed, edited and approved by a person before it goes live, and anything drafted with AI assistance is held to the same standard as everything else.
